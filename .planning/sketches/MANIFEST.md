# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction
Simple, light, and easy to use. Fresh colors with essential details standing out clearly. The vibe is reminiscent of clean enterprise tools like WebexCC or Odoo, ensuring it looks native and professional when embedded inside another application's UI.

## Reference Points
- WebexCC
- Odoo
